Who I Am

I'm a BSIT student specializing in cyber security, working toward a career as a security analyst. Under the SJ Developing name, I build security tools that find weak spots, protect data, and stop people from getting tricked: vulnerability scanners, encryption tools, and phishing defenses.

Because I also build full-stack apps with Node.js, Next.js, and FastAPI, I understand how the code I'm trying to secure is actually written. My programming roots go back to 2023, when I developed games on Roblox using Lua — building something from scratch and seeing people interact with it is what sparked my love for development.

When I'm not studying or coding, I'm reading security write-ups, experimenting with tools in my own lab, and sharpening my skills to become the kind of analyst who builds things people can actually trust.
